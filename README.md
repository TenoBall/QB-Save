# QB-Save
Determine NFL fourth quarter stats when a QB has the ball with a lead

## Definitions
The sample space is when a QB has the ball in the fourth quarter with a one possession lead. Winning QBs will put their team ahead two possessions to ice the game.  

-QB Save - Drive that starts in 4th quarter ends in a score to put the team up another possession or completion of the game. An exception here would be on a meaningless kneel down drive.  

-QB Hold - Drive that starts in 4th quarter where you score but it only keeps the team up one possession.  

-Thank Your Defense - Drive that starts in 4th quarter where offense gives the ball back and the other team does not take the lead.  

-Blown Save - Drive that starts in 4th quarter where offense gives the ball back and the other team scores to take the lead.
